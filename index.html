Roll, race and outrun giant boss balls across eight wild worlds.

What's inside

8 worlds

Meadows, ocean bays, canyons, snow peaks, sky islands, a neon city, a volcano and a space station, each with its own scenery and music.

Boss chases

At the end of every world a giant ball rolls after you. Keep moving or get flattened.

Wild tracks

Loops, glass tubes, half-pipes, slamming hammers, swinging platforms, brick walls, ice and conveyor belts.

Gems and power-ups

Grab risky gems, then magnets, shields and double coins. Land on x5 at the bonus ramp.

Races and ghosts

Race rival marbles, and chase a ghost of your own best run on every level.

Unlock your style

22 ball skins, 9 trails and 5 finish celebrations, plus trophies and a daily reward.

How to play

Hold anywhere to roll. Slide sideways to steer. Pull your thumb down to stop or go backwards. On a computer, use the arrow keys.
