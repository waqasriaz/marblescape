Privacy Policy

Marble Scape · Last updated 9 October 2026

Marble Scape is a game made by Waqas Riaz. This page explains what the game does with information about you. In short, it doesn't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send any information about you or your device to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress is saved only on your phone, so you can carry on where you left off. That covers levels reached, coins, the skins and items you've unlocked, trophies, best times and settings. It never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds something that changes this (for example online leaderboards), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
